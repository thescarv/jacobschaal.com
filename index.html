Jacob Schaal

Economist · AI & Labour Markets

I am an economist researching how AI affects junior hiring, job tasks and career ladders. As a Research Fellow at the AI Objectives Institute, I work with Dr Bouke Klein Teeselink at King’s College London on how exposure to AI changes junior hiring, using UK job-ad data and a triple-difference design. I hold an MSc in Economics from the London School of Economics.

Generative AI is good at the codifiable tasks that junior staff learn on. I study what this means for entry-level work and what policy should do about it. With Alex Imas, I recently reviewed the evidence on AI and the labour market, and I wrote the labour market section of A Transformative AI Strategy for Europe. My writing has appeared in the Frankfurter Allgemeine Zeitung, Handelsblatt, Transformer and AI Frontiers, and Bloomberg has covered my proposal for financing entry-level training.

Previously, I was AI Governance Research Manager at Cambridge ERA and co-edited Windfall Trust’s AI Economics Brief.
